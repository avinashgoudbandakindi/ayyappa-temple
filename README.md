# 🕉️ Sacred Ayyappa Swamy Temple Portal

An elevated, spiritually divine, and responsive digital temple portal dedicated to **Lord Ayyappa Swamy (Hari-Hara Putra)**. Built with modern web standards, classical Vedic aesthetics, interactive spiritual features, and online devotee services.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Modern Enhancements

### 1. 🪔 Sacred Vedic Aesthetic & Color Palette
- **Antique Temple Gold (`#D4AF37`, `#F5C842`)**: Shimmering gopuram and foil accents.
- **Sacred Saffron & Kesari (`#FF6F00`, `#E65100`)**: Traditional marigold and devotion motifs.
- **Temple Kumkum & Vermilion (`#B71C1C`)**: Auspicious ceremonies and e-hundi offerings.
- **Sanctum Obsidian Nocturnal Mode (`#0C0A07`)**: A dark Garbhagriha mode with warm diya-glow lighting and zero contrast issues.
- **Ivory & Sandalwood Mode (`#FAF7F0`)**: Pristine South Indian marble stone daytime appearance.

### 2. 🏛️ Signature Features & Interactive Components
- **Top Auspicious Ticker & Live Darshan Status**: Real-time temple status pill, daily Tithi, Nakshatram, and Rahu Kalam timings.
- **Web Audio API Sacred Chimes & Om Drone**: Realistic acoustic brass temple bells and tanpura resonance with harmonic synthesis (works 100% offline).
- **Pathinettam Padi (18 Sacred Steps) Interactive Explorer**:
  - Educational and spiritual guide through all 18 holy steps.
  - Category filters: *The 5 Senses*, *The 8 Vices (Ashta Ragam)*, *The 3 Gunas*, and *Vidya & Moksha*.
  - Step-by-step Sanskrit mantra, meaning, and virtue display.
- **E-Seva & Online Pooja Booking System**:
  - Interactive cards for *Neyyabhishekam*, *Pushpabhishekam*, *Maha Padi Pooja*, *Nitya Annadanam*, *Sahasranama Archana*, and *Udayasthamana Pooja*.
  - Clean booking modal with devotee details (Gothram, Nakshatram, preferred date) and instant confirmation.
- **Auspicious E-Hundi (Donation System)**:
  - Preset Vedic denominations: `₹108` (Japa), `₹501` (Shagun), `₹1,008` (Sahasra), `₹5,001` (Annadanam), `₹11,000` (Patron).
  - 80G Indian Income Tax exemption notice and multi-purpose fund allocation.
- **Curated High-Resolution Imagery**:
  - High-definition authentic photography of Lord Ayyappa idol, illuminated twilight temple, 18 golden steps, evening deeparadhana aarti, and banana-leaf Annadanam feast.
- **Filterable Gallery with Lightbox**: Smooth category filtering and fullscreen view.
- **Devotee Voices & Community Testimonials**: Interactive experience sharing with instant UI update and toast notifications.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5**: Semantic markup, meta tags for SEO, and accessibility attributes.
- **CSS3**: CSS Custom Properties (Variables), Fluid Typography (`clamp`), Glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter`), CSS Grid, and Keyframe Animations.
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Modular functions, Web Audio API oscillator synthesis, LocalStorage theme persistence, and dynamic DOM manipulation.
- **Google Fonts**: `Cinzel` (Majestic Headings), `Marcellus` & `Rozha One` (Devanagari/Mantras), `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Readable modern body text).
- **Font Awesome 6.5**: Crisp vector icons for religious and functional UI elements.
