About Sree Ayyappa Bhaktha Samithi

Serving devotees with unwavering faith, humility, and adherence to Sanatana Dharma

Located in the heart of Hyderabad on Raj Bhavan Road, Somajiguda, Sree Ayyappa Swamy Temple stands as an oasis of spiritual tranquility. Established by the revered Sree Ayyappa Bhaktha Samithi, the temple serves hundreds of pilgrims annually, especially during the austere 41-day Mandala-Makaravilakku pilgrimage season.

In full accordance with South Indian Agamic traditions and Kerala Tantric rituals, our sanctum enshrines Lord Ayyappa in yogic posture, flanked by Lord Maha Ganapathi and Sri Durga Ammavaru.

Temple Etiquette & Dress Code

  • Devotees must wear traditional Indian attire (Men: Dhoti/Kurta; Women: Saree/Salwar).
  • Please deposit footwear at the designated counter outside the temple entrance.
  • Photography inside the inner sanctum is strictly prohibited. Keep mobile phones silent.

Parking & Transit

Dedicated parking space for two-wheelers and limited cars is available near the temple lane. During festival days, devotees are encouraged to utilize public transit or arrive early.

Devotee Accessibility

The temple courtyard and main mandapam provide ramp access for elderly devotees and wheelchairs. Priority darshan assistance is provided by temple volunteers upon request at the office.
